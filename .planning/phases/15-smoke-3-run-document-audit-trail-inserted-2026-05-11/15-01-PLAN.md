|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-01 | Tampering | renderer-supplied `data.calculationRulesVersion` payload could be set to arbitrary strings | accept | Marker is informational only — renderer treats anything `!== 'smoke3'` as legacy and renders the banner. | T-15-03 | Tampering | renderer could inject arbitrary numeric values into `sapeConcentration`, `beadsVolumePerWell`, etc. that don't match the master panel | accept | Snapshot-at-save is by design (D-15-01) — the renderer IS the source of truth at save time.
