4. **Wizard step 1 — Platform & Species**: pick a platform (e.g. Milliplex) and species. Next button enables.

5. **Wizard step 2 — Analytes**: pick a premix panel OR toggle at least one single analyte. Selections render in sidebar.

   - Hamilton: 1

   - Standard Position: 1
   - Trough Position: 1

9. **Edit-warning modal**: click wizard Back button. Expect modal "This run is saved. Going back to edit will modify the saved record. Continue?" with "Keep viewing" (primary) and "Edit anyway" (destructive). Click "Edit anyway" → returns to step 4.

14. **"Start New Run"** (on Finalized View): click. All stores reset. Wizard returns to step 1. Operator-dropdown + RunList still populated (those are app-wide state).
