## Decisions Made
- **useRef for drag state:** isDragging, dragStart, dragAction stored in refs to avoid re-renders during mouse movement. Only hoveredWells uses useState for visual preview.
- **Hook does not own selection state:** Delegates to onSelectionChange callback, allowing plateStore to remain source of truth.
- **finalizeDrag reads via setState callback:** Avoids stale closure when reading hoveredWells during window mouseup.
- **Interactive mode detection:** PlateGrid checks for presence of selectedWells or hoveredWells props to determine interactive vs read-only rendering.
